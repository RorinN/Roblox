# Ideas

A parking place for everything we think of but are not building yet.

**How this works:** Robin and Claude brainstorm in chat. Anything worth keeping
lands here. Claude pushes it, Robin tells the building session *"git pull, then
read docs/ideas.md"*. Nothing gets re-typed.

**The one rule:** nothing in this file gets built until Pet Yeet v1 is published
and has real players. A live game tells us which of these ideas were actually
good — and we will both guess wrong about roughly half of them.

---

## For Pet Yeet, after launch

Pulled from the design doc's v2 list. Not ranked yet; ranking happens once we
can see what players actually do.

- **Rebirths** — reset progress for a permanent multiplier. The standard
  simulator retention loop, and the most proven of everything here.
- **Egg hatching** — the thing Robin originally reached for. It would also give
  the dropped "luck" gamepass something to act on.
- **Pet fusion** — combine duplicates into stronger pets.
- **Trading** — player-to-player. Powerful, but it brings scams and duplication
  exploits with it. Not a casual addition.
- **More maps** — new launch sites with different looks and modifiers.
- **Player interaction** — seeing other people's throws, racing a friend.
- **Replay and cinematic camera** — a clean no-HUD replay of your best throw, a
  slow-motion beat on the perfect release. Cut from v1 purely for time; this is
  the one most directly aimed at the clips that drive Roblox virality.
- **Personal-best cards** — a shareable image of your record throw.
- **Custom audio** — blocked until Robin's account is confirmed age-verified.
  Until then the free Roblox audio library is the design constraint, not a
  limitation to work around.

## Future games

- ***Don't Drop The Egg*** — carry a physics egg through an obstacle course while
  other players sabotage you. Already decided as the next project. Deliberately
  second because it needs a populated server to be fun, which Pet Yeet will
  supply.

## Considered and rejected — with reasons

Kept so we do not re-argue settled questions.

- **Real physics throwing** — inconsistent across the network, painful to tune,
  cheat-prone, and worst on phones. The whole reason v1 uses a calculated arc.
- **A "luck" gamepass in v1** — there is no randomness in v1 for it to act on.
  Revisit only if egg hatching ships.
- **Egg-themed game for v1** — the egg meta is at peak saturation; *Steal An Egg*
  peaked at 7.6 million players. The durable pattern is the *shape* — one verb,
  collection, clips — not eggs.
